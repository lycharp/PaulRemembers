Privacy Policy

PaulRemembers, by PoquitoWare. Last updated November 2, 2026.

PaulRemembers is a personal journal app. This page explains, in plain language, what information it handles and where that information goes. We do not sell your information, show you ads, or share your journal with anyone you have not chosen to share it with.

Your journal entries

Everything you write, plus the photos, videos and audio you add, is stored in your web browser on your own computer or phone. If you never sign in, your journal stays on that device and is never sent to us.

Signing in and syncing

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, your journals, entries and attachments are copied to our cloud database (hosted by Supabase) so they can sync between your devices. Only your account can read them, apart from anything you choose to share (see below).

Google sign-in

If you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive only your name, email address and Google profile picture, which Google provides with the basic sign-in permission. We use them only to identify your account and show who is signed in. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data. Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

You can share a journal with other people by adding their email address; they can then see it after they sign in. You can also share a single entry with a link. Anyone who has that link can view that entry, so only send it to people you trust. You can stop sharing at any time, after which the link stops working.

Other services the app talks to

  • Location: if you tap the button to add your current location, your browser asks your permission, and the coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look up the name of the place. Nothing else is sent with them.
  • Audio transcription: turning a recording into text happens entirely on your device. The first time you use it, the speech model is downloaded from Hugging Face; your recordings are never uploaded.

Deleting your information

You can delete entries and journals inside the app at any time. Deleting while signed in also removes them from the cloud. To delete your account and everything stored with it, contact us using the link below and we will remove it.

Children

PaulRemembers is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
