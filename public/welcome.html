Paul Remembers

A private journal app by PoquitoWare

Paul Remembers is a personal journal you can use on Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android, right in your web browser. Write about your day, add photos, videos, voice recordings and places, and look back on your memories later.

What you can do

  • Keep one or more journals, each with dated entries.
  • Add photos, videos, audio recordings and the place you were.
  • Bring in entries exported from Apple's Journal app.
  • Sync your journals across your devices after you sign in.
  • Share a journal with family or friends you choose, or share a single entry with a link.
  • Make backups of your journal to a folder of your choice.

Who can use it

Paul Remembers is invite-only. You sign in with your email address or with your Google account. When you sign in with Google, the app receives only your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to identify your account. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
